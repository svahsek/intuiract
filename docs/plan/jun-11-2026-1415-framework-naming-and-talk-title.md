# Framework Naming and Talk Title

## What This Framework Actually Is (in one sentence)

**Human expertise encoded as machine-readable standards, executed by a stateless AI every time.**

That's the core insight. Not "AI documentation." Not "automated docs." The intelligence is human — trained over years of technical communication practice. AI is the executor, not the author.

That distinction is what the name needs to capture.

---

## What to Avoid and Why

| Word | Problem |
|---|---|
| "Automation" | Implies replacing humans. Misrepresents what this is. |
| "AI-assisted" | Too casual. Every tool is AI-assisted now. Says nothing. |
| "Smart docs" | Consumer-grade. Not serious enough for org identity. |
| "DocOps" | Already an existing discipline with its own meaning. |
| "Intelligent" alone | Vague. Doesn't say where the intelligence comes from. |

---

## Four Serious Candidates

**1. Governed Documentation Intelligence**
The word "governed" is the key differentiator — it says the intelligence is ruled by human-encoded standards, not free-form AI generation. Strong for an org that cares about compliance, consistency, and auditability. Shortens to **GDI**.

**2. Structured Documentation Intelligence**
Connects directly to the heritage of structured authoring, DITA, topic-based writing — disciplines that technical communication built and this framework extends into the AI era. Says: we didn't abandon what technical communication built, we operationalised it. Shortens to **SDI**.

**3. Documentation Intelligence Architecture**
Architecture signals that this is designed, not improvised. It's what someone builds, not something they stumbled into. Connects to information architecture as a real TC discipline. Strong for positioning the talk as a systems-thinking contribution.

**4. Encoded Documentation Intelligence**
Most literal — expertise encoded, AI reads it. Very accurate. Slightly academic-sounding, which works well in a professional context. Shortens to **EDI**.

---

## Recommendation

**Structured Documentation Intelligence (SDI)**

Because it does three things in three words:
- **Structured** — honours decades of technical communication practice (structured authoring, single-sourcing, topic-based writing). Signals the field's foundations were not abandoned.
- **Documentation** — unambiguous about the domain.
- **Intelligence** — signals AI involvement but implies it is directed and governed, not autonomous.

---

## Talk Title Options

**Full version:**
> Structured Documentation Intelligence: Encoding Human Expertise for AI-Executed Documentation

**Short version:**
> Structured Documentation Intelligence: When Standards Are the Brain and AI Is the Hand

---

## The Line That Will Land in the Talk

*"Standards are the brain. AI is the hand."*

This reframes the entire conversation about AI in documentation — from "will AI replace writers" to "how do expert writers make AI do exactly what they would do." That is the announcement worth making.
