# UX/UI Team and AI Tools — Talking Points

> Crisp bullet format. Use as speaker notes or explain each point in your own words.

---

## The Shift

- UX/UI team previously used **Figma** as the only design tool
- Now using a **combination of AI tools** alongside Figma
- Not a replacement — an acceleration of the design process

---

## How They Use Each Tool

**Figma** — still the foundation
- All complex UX flows, design systems, and final handoffs happen here
- Unchanged as the source of truth

**Claude** — for complex app design
- Think through user journeys and UX rationale faster
- Challenges assumptions, surfaces alternatives early
- Paired with Figma for deeper, multi-screen products

**Lovable / Bolt.new** — for quick turnarounds
- Websites, microsites, landing pages
- Go from a brief to a working visual prototype in hours, not days
- Used when speed matters more than deep UX complexity

---

## The Impact

- **[X]% time saved** on early-stage design work
- Faster iteration → more time for quality thinking
- Designers spend less time on scaffolding, more time on decisions

---

## What This Is Not

- Not AI replacing designers
- Team still owns: UX guidelines, accessibility standards, visual language, every design decision
- AI removes friction — blank canvas problem, first-draft time, scaffolding
- **Designer's judgment is irreplaceable. AI speeds up everything around it.**

---

## One Line to Leave With

> The highest value a designer brings is judgment — knowing what is right for the user. AI accelerates everything around that judgment.
